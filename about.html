reverist

(n.) rev-er-ist. One who gets lost in thought.

The Reverist is one line at a time, with nothing else on the page, because a good line deserves a moment of quiet around it. Every line here is written by Ankit Mehta.

New lines appear here as they are posted on Instagram, several times a day, with something special on festivals and meaningful days.

One line every Sunday

Get a single line in your inbox each Sunday, and nothing else. Email us to subscribe, and reply “stop” at any time to leave.

Take a line with you

Every line can be shared, saved as an Instagram post or a phone wallpaper, or kept with the heart so you can find it again in the archive. In the evening the site turns dark; in the day it turns light.
