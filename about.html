reverist

(n.) rev-er-ist. One who gets lost in thought.

The Reverist collects short lines that make you stop. One at a time, with nothing else on the page, because a good line deserves a moment of quiet around it.

Some lines have travelled a long way and nobody remembers who said them first. Others are written here, and those carry their author's name.

One line every Sunday

Get a single line in your inbox each Sunday, and nothing else. Email us to subscribe, and reply "stop" at any time to leave.

Send us a line

Written something that made you stare at a wall? Send it to us. The best ones are posted with your name.

Take a line with you

Every line on the home page can be saved as an Instagram post or a phone wallpaper. In the evening the site turns dark; in the day it turns light. You can switch it yourself at the top of the page.
